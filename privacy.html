最后更新：2026-05-28

中文（简体）

抽烟记录是一款用于记录吸烟时间、查看近期记录与统计趋势的工具应用。

我们不会收集你的个人信息，不会追踪你，也不会将你的数据出售、出租或共享给第三方用于广告或营销。

你在应用中创建的吸烟记录数据仅保存在你的设备本地。应用不要求注册账号，不会把记录上传到我们的服务器。

应用可能使用 Apple 系统提供的本地存储能力来保存记录。卸载应用或清除本地数据可能会删除这些记录。

如需咨询隐私相关问题，请发送邮件至 xuhuancoderworld@gmail.com。

English

Smoke Record is a utility app for recording smoking times, viewing recent records, and checking simple statistics.

We do not collect personal information, do not track you, and do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

The smoking records you create in the app are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not upload your records to our servers.

The app may use local storage provided by Apple systems to save records. Uninstalling the app or clearing local data may remove these records.

If you have privacy questions, contact us at xuhuancoderworld@gmail.com.
